Writing an "alternatives" page about Pointing Poker is a slightly humbling exercise, because Pointing Poker is really hard to argue with. It's free, there's no account, no download, and no participant ceiling. You open a room, share the URL, you estimate. Millions of votes a month, funded entirely by some ads.

Full disclosure, since this page ranks for a query with obvious buying intent and we'd look silly pretending otherwise: we make a competing tool. But we're not going to sit here and tell you the free thing you're happily using is secretly bad. It isn't.

If you're on this page, it's almost certainly not because Pointing Poker is bad. It's because you've bumped into one of its deliberate limits:

- **Nothing connects to your backlog.** Every ticket title is retyped by hand, and every agreed estimate is transcribed back into Jira manually.
- **Session history is thin.** If you want to look back at what the team estimated last sprint, or track how estimates compared to reality, the data isn't really there.
- **Ads.** Some organizations simply won't run an ad-supported tool in a work meeting.
- **No video call integration.** Everyone leaves the call view to go vote.

The trade-off you're making is clear: Pointing Poker is free because it does less. Here's what you get if you're willing to give up "completely free."

### If ads are the only problem

Go to **Scrum Poker Online**. It's free, open source, ad-free, has no participant limit, and needs no account — essentially Pointing Poker without the ads. It also supports QR code room sharing, and you can self-host it if your security team wants the data inside your own infrastructure.

This is the smallest possible move and it costs nothing. If ads are your sole complaint, stop here.

### If you want your backlog connected

This is the real reason teams outgrow Pointing Poker, and it's the one worth paying for.

**PlanningPoker.live** connects to Jira and Linear in both directions: pull issues into the session, estimate as a team, and write the story points back. It also exports the whole session to CSV, which is what you want if you're tracking estimate accuracy over time or working with a tracker we don't integrate with — including [Azure DevOps](/knowledge-base/planning-poker-azure-devops).

**PlanningPoker.com** and **Parabol** also offer live Jira integration. **PlanITpoker** does file-based import from Jira XML and CSV, which is better than nothing but still manual.

The honest framing: manual entry costs roughly five to ten minutes per session. If you refine fortnightly, that's about two hours a year — which may genuinely not be worth paying to avoid. If you refine twice a week with a large backlog, it's a different calculation entirely.

### If estimation happens inside a video call

Pointing Poker is a browser tab, so everyone leaves the meeting view to use it. **PlanningPoker.live** runs as an embedded app in Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and Webex, keeping the session inside the call.

For distributed teams this is usually a bigger day-to-day improvement than any integration.

### What you'd be giving up

The catch, stated plainly rather than tucked into a footnote: **moving off Pointing Poker means you stop being free.**

PlanningPoker.live charges a credit per room created. You get 5 at signup and 1 free each month, and bundles are one-time — $9 for 7 rooms, $17 for 15, $50 for 50. Joining is always free, so participants never pay and there's no per-seat maths. Starter credits expire after two months and carry ads; purchased ones do neither.

For a scrum master running fortnightly refinement, a $17 bundle covers most of a year. If that still reads as worse than free for your situation, then **stay on Pointing Poker.** It's a good tool, you're already happy, and "the free option was correct" is a perfectly respectable outcome for a page like this one.

### The bottom line

**Ads are your only issue?** Scrum Poker Online. Free, open source, one-minute switch.

**You want your backlog connected and your sessions recorded?** PlanningPoker.live if you use Jira or Linear and estimate during a call; PlanningPoker.com or Parabol if you want estimation bundled with broader agile tooling.

**Nothing above is bothering you?** Then don't switch. Pointing Poker is genuinely good at what it does.
